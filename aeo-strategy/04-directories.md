# Top 10 Directories & Citation Sources for AI/AEO Authority

**Date:** 2026-04-29
**Goal:** Build the off-site footprint that AI engines (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews) use to decide whether to cite Tennessee Cash For Homes as a trusted source.

**Why these matter more than regular SEO:** AI engines validate businesses by *cross-checking* claims across multiple independent sources. A business that says "we have an A+ BBB rating" is much more likely to be cited if BBB.org *also* confirms it, Yelp confirms the existence, Trustpilot has reviews, etc. The goal here is **citation density** — being the same business mentioned consistently across many trusted sources.

## 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) — ★★★★★

**Why:** GBP is the single biggest local SEO and AEO signal. AI Overviews and Google's SGE pull directly from GBP data (categories, services, hours, reviews, Q&A, posts).

**How to claim/optimize:**
1. Go to https://business.google.com and sign in with the business email (`info@tncashforhomes.com`).
2. Search for "Tennessee Cash For Homes" — if a profile exists, click "Claim this business."
3. Verify via postcard, phone, or email (Google chooses).
4. Set:
   - **Primary category:** "Real Estate Agency" (or "Real Estate Investing Service" if visible in your region)
   - **Secondary categories:** "We Buy Houses," "Real Estate Consultant"
   - **Service area:** Add all major TN cities (Nashville, Memphis, Knoxville, Chattanooga, Murfreesboro, Franklin, Clarksville, Spring Hill, etc.) — at least 20.
   - **Services:** Add "Cash home buying," "Sell house fast," "We buy houses as-is," "Foreclosure help," "Probate / inherited home buying," "Rental property purchase"
   - **Description (750 chars):** Use the same intro paragraph you have on the about page.
   - **Hours:** 24/7 (matches your schema)
5. Upload at least 25 photos: team, branded vehicle, 5+ before/after sold homes, exterior signage if any.
6. Pre-seed the **Q&A** section with the 6 FAQs from `03-aeo-faq-content.md` — answer each from the business account.
7. **Posts:** publish 1–2 GBP posts per week (sold-home updates, FAQ answers, area highlights).
8. **Reviews:** ask every closed seller for a Google review — and reply to every one within 48 hours, mentioning the city.

## Operational tips

- **Use one canonical NAP everywhere.** Pick *one* exact format and apply it on every listing:
  ```
  Name:    Tennessee Cash For Homes
  Address: 4183 Franklin Rd, Murfreesboro, TN 37128
  Phone:   (615) 801-8126
  Website: https://www.tennesseecashforhomes.com
  ```
  Even a comma difference can fragment your citation graph.
- **As you create profiles, update `sameAs` in `functions.php`** (line 580 area) to add each new URL. That tells search/AI engines "yes, all these profiles are the same business."
- **Track your citation graph.** Run a free Moz Local check or a manual quarterly audit: search `"Tennessee Cash For Homes" "(615) 801-8126"` and count distinct domains that appear.
- **Don't fake reviews on any of these.** AI engines specifically look for natural review distribution patterns, and platforms penalize businesses for fake reviews more aggressively now than they did even 2 years ago.
