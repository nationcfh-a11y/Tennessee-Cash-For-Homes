- ❌ No guest posts on industry blogs (BiggerPockets, Inman, etc.)

**How to fix:**

  - Local NPR affiliates (WPLN Nashville)

### 🟡 Priority 2 — Experience signals (deepen what's already there)

#### A. Customer reviews are short and generic
The reviews on the homepage are real, but most are 1–2 sentences ("Great company, highly recommend"). AI engines weight long, detailed, specific reviews much higher.

**How to fix:**
- When asking past sellers for a Google review, give them a prompt: *"What was the situation with the property, what was your timeline, and how did the process compare to what you expected?"* The 200-word reviews are far more citable than the 20-word ones.
- Aim for **at least 3–5 reviews longer than 100 words** in the next 90 days.

#### B. No case studies or before/after content
- ❌ No "we closed a foreclosure case in 9 days" case study with real numbers
- ❌ No before/after photos of homes you've bought and resold
- ❌ No video testimonials

**How to fix (in priority order):**
1. **Case study post:** Pick one closed deal where the seller is willing to be on camera (or in writing). Write up: "Mary in Clarksville faced a foreclosure auction in 14 days. Here's how we closed in 9. (With Mary's permission and a video at the end.)" That single piece of content outperforms 50 generic blog posts for AEO.
2. **Before/after photos** on every interior page where it makes sense. Add 6–10 to the about page or a new "Recent Sales" page.
3. **Video testimonials:** Even 3 short (60–90 sec) videos make a huge difference. Upload to YouTube, embed on your homepage, transcript in `VideoObject` schema.

The about page hero says **"For over 9 years, Tennessee Cash For Homes has helped homeowners…"** but Karson's bio says he has **"nearly 3 years of hands-on real estate experience."** These can both be true (the company is 9 years old; Karson joined more recently), but a reader — and an AI engine — will flag the apparent contradiction.

### 🟢 Priority 4 — Trustworthiness fine-tuning

#### A. Privacy policy and disclosures
- ✅ You have a `page-privacy-policy.php` — good.
- ❓ Verify it explicitly addresses: how you handle property data submitted via the form, your relationship with any title companies, and whether you re-sell leads (don't, but say so).

#### B. Refund / cancellation policy
- ❌ Most cash buyer sites don't have one, and they should. Sellers want to know they can back out before closing.

**How to fix:**
- Add a short "Seller Protections" section to the FAQ or how-it-works page that says: *"You may cancel any signed agreement without penalty up to [X] days before closing. We will refund any earnest money held in escrow if you do."* (Adjust to match your actual contract terms.)

#### C. Make the BBB badge more prominent
The BBB Accreditation is visible in the about page credentials box but isn't on the homepage hero or the form area.

**How to fix:**
- Add the BBB Accredited Business badge (downloadable from BBB.org) directly next to the lead form on the homepage. Trust badges next to a CTA reduce form abandonment AND give AI engines a visual confirmation.
