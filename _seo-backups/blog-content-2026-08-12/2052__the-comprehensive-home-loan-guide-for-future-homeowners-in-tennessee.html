Fixed-Rate vs. Adjustable-Rate Mortgages: A Stability Versus Flexibility Debate

The choice between a fixed-rate mortgage (FRM) and an adjustable-rate mortgage (ARM) often boils down to your preference for stability in your monthly payments versus potentially lower rates (and payments) that ARMs can initially provide. FRMs lock in your interest rate for the life of the loan, offering predictable monthly payments. In contrast, ARMs may start with lower rates but adjust over time, which can be advantageous for those planning a shorter stay in their home.
