Housing Costs

One of the most significant factors in the cost of living is housing. In Clarksville, the real estate market is diverse, offering a range of options from historic homes to modern developments. According to Zillow, the median home value in Clarksville is approximately $230,000 as of 2023, which is slightly below the national average. For renters, the average monthly rent for a two-bedroom apartment is around $1,100 to $1,300, depending on the location and amenities.

Utility Expenses

Utilities are another crucial aspect of living expenses. Residents of Clarksville can expect to pay around $150 to $200 per month for basic utilities such as electricity, water, and gas. Internet and cable services typically add an additional $60 to $100 to monthly expenses.

Transportation Costs

Transportation is an essential factor to consider, especially in a city like Clarksville, where public transportation options are limited. The majority of residents rely on personal vehicles, with the average cost of gasoline in the area being slightly below the national average. Car insurance rates can vary based on several factors, but they typically range from $1,200 to $1,500 annually.

Food and Groceries

Grocery prices in Clarksville are comparable to the national average. A typical household can expect to spend around $300 to $400 per month on groceries. Dining out is also a popular option, with a meal at a mid-range restaurant costing approximately $15 to $20 per person.

Healthcare Costs

Healthcare is a vital but often variable expense. In Tennessee, the average cost of health insurance is about $440 per month for an individual plan. Out-of-pocket medical expenses can vary widely based on individual health needs and insurance coverage.

Education and Childcare

For families with children, education and childcare are significant considerations. The average cost of childcare in Clarksville ranges from $600 to $1,000 per month, depending on the type of care and the child's age. Public schools in Clarksville are generally well-regarded, and there are also several private school options with varying tuition rates.

Entertainment and Recreation

Clarksville offers a variety of entertainment and recreational activities, from exploring the city's rich history to enjoying its parks and outdoor spaces. While the costs can vary, residents can enjoy a range of activities without breaking the bank. For example, a movie ticket costs around $10 to $12, and a monthly gym membership is typically between $20 and $40.

Overall Cost of Living

According to the Cost of Living Index, Clarksville's overall cost of living is slightly below the national average, making it an affordable option for many individuals and families. The city's growing economy and diverse range of amenities make it an attractive place to live, work, and play.
