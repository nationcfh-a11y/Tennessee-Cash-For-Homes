What the 2025 Housing Market Looks Like in Tennessee

Tennessee’s real estate market in 2025 is best described as complex but promising. Over the last several years, home values across the state have climbed significantly. In Davidson County, which includes Nashville, property values have surged nearly 45 percent since 2021 according to Axios. Homeowners in other parts of the state have also seen strong gains, making now an attractive time to consider selling.

However, rising home values do not guarantee a quick or easy sale. Inventory is also rising. According to data from Houzeo, the number of homes for sale in Tennessee has jumped by 16.8 percent compared to the previous year. This means more competition for sellers and more choices for buyers. As a result, homes are sitting on the market longer than they were just a couple of years ago. The current average is about 75 days, up significantly from the faster pace seen during the peak of the pandemic market.

How Interest Rates Are Influencing Buyer Decisions

Mortgage interest rates are another important factor shaping the real estate landscape. As of early 2025, rates are holding steady between 6.21 percent and 6.99 percent. Buyers are now taking more time to compare properties and are more sensitive to price and condition.

This change in behavior affects how sellers should prepare and price their homes. Sellers who want to attract motivated buyers in this environment need to be strategic.
