Understanding Distressed Houses

A distressed house is a property that is under financial or physical distress. This could be due to foreclosure, bankruptcy, or the house requiring significant repairs. According to the National Association of Realtors, distressed home sales accounted for approximately 3% of total home sales in the United States in 2021.

Challenges of Selling a Distressed House

Selling a distressed house through traditional methods presents several challenges:

  • Lengthy Process: Traditional home sales can take months or even years, which is not ideal for homeowners facing financial difficulties or needing to sell quickly.
  • Repairs and Renovations: Distressed houses often require significant repairs, which can be costly and time-consuming.
  • Lower Market Value: Due to their condition, distressed houses typically sell for less than market value, reducing potential profits for the seller.
  • Difficulty in Finding Buyers: Many buyers are hesitant to purchase distressed properties due to the associated risks and uncertainties.
