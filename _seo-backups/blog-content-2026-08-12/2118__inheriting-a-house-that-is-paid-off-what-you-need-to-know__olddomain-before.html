Inheritance and Tax Implications:

In Tennessee, there is no inheritance tax on inheriting a house, a favorable aspect compared to other states. The Internal Revenue Service (IRS) establishes a new Fair Market Value (FMV) for the inherited property. This FMV affects fees when selling the house, potentially impacting tax liabilities.

Steps to Take When You Inherit a House:

  1. Pay off any associated debts or mortgages.
  2. Discuss plans with other co-owners to determine the property's future.
  3. Consider investing in repairs to enhance the property's value, even if selling is the ultimate goal.

Tax Considerations on Inherited Property:

Inheriting a house itself does not trigger immediate tax liability. However, actions taken with the property, such as moving in, renting, or selling, can result in various taxes, including capital gains and property taxes.

Home Sale Tax Exclusion:

To qualify for the Home Sale Tax Exclusion, living in the inherited house for a minimum of two years is necessary. Reinvesting the money from the sale into a new home can exempt it from taxation.

Inheriting Real Estate with Siblings:

Inheriting real estate with siblings is common but can lead to complications, particularly if disagreements arise. Siblings may clash on issues such as purchase price and repairs. Seeking knowledge and resources to navigate potential conflicts becomes crucial.
