The Drawbacks of Traditional Home Sales

While listing with a real estate agent remains a popular choice for many, it comes with significant downsides. Sellers must prepare their homes for showings, make necessary repairs, and navigate negotiations that can delay the process. For those in urgent situations, traditional home sales can be overwhelming. This uncertainty makes cash sales a more appealing alternative for homeowners who need a straightforward solution.
