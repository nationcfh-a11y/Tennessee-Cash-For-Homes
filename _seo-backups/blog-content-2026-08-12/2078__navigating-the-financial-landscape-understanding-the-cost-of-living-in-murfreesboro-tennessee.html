Murfreesboro, Tennessee, is a city that has been attracting attention for its vibrant community, rich history, and promising economic growth. As more people consider calling Murfreesboro their home, understanding the cost of living in this city becomes essential. In this comprehensive guide, we'll break down the various expenses you can expect when living in Murfreesboro, providing you with a clearer picture of what to budget for in this dynamic Tennessee city.

Housing Costs

One of the most significant factors in the cost of living is housing. According to Zillow, the median home value in Murfreesboro is around $330,000 as of 2023, which is slightly higher than the national average but still reasonable compared to larger metropolitan areas. Renters can expect to pay an average of $1,200 to $1,500 per month for a two-bedroom apartment, depending on the location and amenities.

Utility Expenses

Utility costs in Murfreesboro are generally on par with the national average. Residents can expect to pay around $150 to $200 per month for electricity, water, and gas combined. Internet and cable services typically add an additional $60 to $100 to monthly expenses.

Transportation Costs

Transportation is another crucial aspect of the cost of living. Murfreesboro is a car-dependent city, with most residents relying on personal vehicles for commuting. The average cost of gasoline in Murfreesboro is slightly below the national average, at around $2.50 per gallon. Car insurance rates vary depending on various factors but generally range from $1,200 to $1,500 annually.

Food and Groceries

Grocery prices in Murfreesboro are comparable to the national average. A typical household can expect to spend around $300 to $400 per month on groceries. Dining out is also a popular option, with a meal at a mid-range restaurant costing approximately $15 to $20 per person.

Healthcare Costs

Healthcare is an essential yet often variable expense. The average cost of health insurance in Tennessee is about $440 per month for an individual plan. Out-of-pocket medical expenses can vary widely based on individual health needs and insurance coverage.

Education and Childcare

For families with children, education and childcare are significant considerations. The average cost of childcare in Murfreesboro ranges from $600 to $1,000 per month, depending on the type of care and the child's age. Public schools in Murfreesboro are generally well-regarded, and there are also several private school options with varying tuition rates.

Entertainment and Recreation

Murfreesboro offers a variety of entertainment and recreational activities, from historical sites and parks to shopping and dining. While the costs can vary, residents can enjoy a range of activities without breaking the bank. For example, a movie ticket costs around $10 to $12, and a monthly gym membership is typically between $20 and $40.

Overall Cost of Living

According to the Cost of Living Index, Murfreesboro's overall cost of living is slightly above the national average but still more affordable than many other cities in the United States. The city's growing economy, coupled with its relatively low cost of living, makes it an attractive option for individuals and families looking for a balance between affordability and quality of life.
